# HAPI-eVolved community conduct

This code applies to HAPI-eVolved issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project-managed community spaces. It also applies when someone represents the
project in another space.

- Be respectful of people with different backgrounds, experience and opinions.
- Critique the work with specific evidence. Explain tradeoffs without personal
  attacks, ridicule or assumptions about another person's motives.
- Welcome questions, acknowledge mistakes and respect requests to stop an
  interaction. Disagreement does not justify repeated unwanted contact.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, use sexualized remarks, publish private
  information without consent or deliberately disrupt a discussion.
- Keep contributions relevant. Do not spam, impersonate maintainers or use
  automation to overwhelm other participants.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close or lock discussions,
restrict participation or block accounts. They should consider the impact and
context, explain decisions when appropriate and apply these expectations to
themselves as well as other participants.

## Reporting and reconsideration

For visible disruption that can be addressed without exposing private details,
ask a repository maintainer to review the relevant discussion. Do not repeat
harmful or private content unnecessarily.

For a sensitive concern, use the repository's
[support request form](https://github.com/BGGBTAC/hapi-evolved/issues/new?template=support.yml)
with **Private contact request** and ask only to arrange a private conduct-reporting
channel. Do not name affected people or describe the incident publicly. Verify
the responding person's repository role before sharing details. No dedicated
private conduct-reporting address is currently published.

Participants may ask maintainers to reconsider a moderation decision, using the
same private-channel process when needed. If a concern involves a maintainer and
no independent project contact is available, GitHub's
[abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
is available for behavior on GitHub. This project does not promise an independent
appeals body or a response deadline.

Handle reports discreetly and share personal information only as necessary to
address the concern. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
